# Optional System Archetypes

Use archetypes as prompts for investigation, never as proof.

## Fixes That Fail
A short-term fix reduces a symptom but creates delayed consequences that restore
or worsen it.

## Shifting the Burden
Repeated symptomatic relief weakens investment in the structural solution.

## Limits to Growth
A reinforcing process initially succeeds until a balancing constraint dominates.

## Success to the Successful
Resources flow toward the currently successful path, reinforcing its advantage
and starving alternatives.

## Escalation
Two actors react to each other's state, creating a reinforcing competitive loop.

When an archetype seems to fit, still produce competing hypotheses and a
falsifiable prediction.
